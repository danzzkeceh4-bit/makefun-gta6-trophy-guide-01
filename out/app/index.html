    Roadmap to the platinum

    Difficulty 4/10 · about 200 hours · 1 playthrough · 0 online trophies · cheats disable trophies and saving · no mission replay — keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots + 1 autosave) · free-roam continues after the story.

    1. Phase 1 — Story & optional missions ~60–80 hours

      Play the story and do optional missions as they appear. Scan vehicles and study animals with the WAiNK app as you go — progress counts toward the 100% checklist later. Do the Chapter 1 meditation at Jason's house (see Missables): it is the only thing in the game that can disappear. Everything else can be finished after the story in free-roam.

      Trophies: the 12 story trophies, plus Kenton, Billy, Cal, the arcade game and the first robberies as they unlock.

    2. Phase 2 — 100% completion & challenges ~100 hours

      After the story, the 100% checklist appears (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion). It is roughly 60% of true completion: 70 of 80 challenges, 200 of 277 base-model vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, 300 of 397 stunts, 100 optional missions, all 8 bank vaults and all 39 gang operations. This phase unlocks A Long Road (gold), One Last Thing and most of the activity, robbery and exploration trophies.

      Trophies: A Long Road, Cunning Stunts, The Shootist, Top Fun, Earn Cash, Not Kudos, Maximum Withdrawal, Pest Control, You WAiNKer!, Whip It Good, Don't Touch My Deck, One Last Thing.

    3. Phase 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup ~10 hours

      Finish the 8 stranger strands (Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete and the rest), the remaining character questlines, and whatever misc trophies are left — the UFO easter egg, the cocktail on Ocean Beach, the showroom Banshee, the tank, the plane at VCIA. Then the platinum pops.

      Trophies: Strangers & Freaks, The Truth Is Out There, In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Catch Me If You Can, Challenging Tank, and the last stragglers.

    Roadmap figures and phase structure follow the published PowerPyx trophy roadmap; see Sources.

    Missable trophies

    Good news: Grand Theft Auto VI is almost entirely missable-proof. Story trophies unlock naturally, quest givers return later, optional missions disappear but don't matter for any trophy, and everything can be done in free-roam after the story. There is exactly one thing to do before it goes away.

    Missable assessment follows the published PowerPyx guide (November 2026): only the Chapter 1 meditation is flagged, and even there it is unconfirmed whether it is required for the trophy — do it anyway, in Chapter 1, to be safe.
